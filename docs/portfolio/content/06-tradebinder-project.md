**Body**

Trading cards with someone you've never met means one person ships first and hopes. In TradeBinder, each card goes to the game shop its new owner picks, and the shop releases both only once both have arrived.

*[Clip: tradebinder-swipe]*

Before a proposal goes out, the app shows the value gap and asks the sender to accept it. Fees are itemized up front. There's no subscription: you pay from a trade balance, or once per trade.

*[Figure: tradebinder-trade]*

It's a working prototype on seeded data. Card art and prices come live from the Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall and YGOProDeck. I built it with Expo and React Native in Cursor, with a coding agent, and tested it on my iPhone.
